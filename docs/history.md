# Research history

The project began with an attempt to understand the binary digits of ln 2 through the arithmetic of its rational approximations. It grew into the study of when arithmetic structure forces or prevents normality. This page records the verified checkpoints. The mathematics itself is presented by topic in the [README](../README.md) and the [result index](results.md), independently of this chronology.

Each checkpoint is a commit on which the full [verification](verification.md) passed. The tags are permanent.
