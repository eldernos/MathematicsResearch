## 2. Executive Synthesis of the Determinant Programme

1. **Where determinants add genuine power:**
   - **Global uniformity transfer (Track A):** Growing-dimension orbit Toeplitz determinants $\det G_{N,r}(h)^{1/r}$ capture the exact Lebesgue component $a_* = \inf_{\mu \in C_b(x)} \mu(\mathcal{N}_b)$, are kernel-verified at the finite-algebraic layer (`NormalityChecker/ToeplitzDeterminants.lean`), and yield sharp per-$N$ bounds $\det G_{N,r}(h) \le (1 - |S_N(h)|^2)^{r-1}$ and linear-in-$(r-1)$ perturbation bounds $\|G_{N,r}(h) - I\| \le \pi|h|(r-1) D_N$.
   - **Unconditional one-shot nonvanishing (Track B):** Prime-centered BBP Hankel matrices $H$ achieve $v_p(\det H) = -r$ and $\det H \ne 0$ for *every* prime $p$, dimension $1 \le r \le p$, and integer shift matrix $m$ with zero exceptional indices (`NormalityChecker/HankelDeterminants.lean`), and rank-sensitive local denominator clearing (`B3`) cancels every $\Theta(p^2)$ term identically against $\det C$.
   - **Breaking $b$-power isolation pinning (Track F):** Consecutive $q$-Padé $2 \times 2$ nonvanishing determinants with cyclotomic denominators $D_n$ break the $(\frac{3}{8} + o(1))N^2$ isolation barrier of $b$-power approximants for $P_b = 1/E_b$, jumping its unconditional transition lower bound exponentially from $\gg \log\log N$ (`GF-45`) to $\ge 0.818 \log_2 N$.

2. **Where determinants hit exact mathematical barriers:**
   - **Zero numerator gain in prime-centered BBP Hankel (Track B):** $v_p(\det H) = -r$ is an amplified denominator pole, not a numerator divisibility factor; Uvarov–Christoffel collapses $\det H_c$ algebraically to the 1-D asymmetric Jacobi Padé linear form ($\mu_2^{\mathrm{Hankel}} = 6.178813$), which cannot beat Alladi–Robinson ($4.622101$) and cannot contradict `GF-47` reset countermodels ($5.18$ vs $1.44$ bits/index).
   - **Separation-cost barrier in orbit Toeplitz Gram (Track A):** Any lower bound built solely from separation of rational orbits costs $r(r-1)\log(Q/4)$, leaving only $r = 1$ for $\ln 2$ BBP models and forcing the entire $D^+(\ln 2; 2) < 1$ problem back into the $2 \times 2$ Weyl sum $1 - |S_N(1)|^2$.
   - **Modular positivity / moment-hierarchy ceiling on $E_2^2$ (Track D):** Multilinear sign correlations, gap-indexed Gram matrices, and all PSD/Lasserre/SOS inequalities on upper correlation bounds cannot surpass `GF-43`'s $1/(3\pi)$.
   - **Non-effectivity of Toeplitz schedules (Track A):** By Kleene's Recursion Theorem applied to de Bruijn sequences, no algorithm can compute an optimal schedule $r_e(N) \to \infty$ from a computable program $e$ for $x_e$.
