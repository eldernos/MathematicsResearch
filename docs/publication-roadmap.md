## Detailed candidate profiles

### 1. Disjunctive non-normal multiplicative Lambert series (New Standalone Paper)
- **Headline theorems:**
  - For every prime-support density $\delta \in (0, 1)$ and nonnegative multiplicative coefficient function $c$ meeting the local hypotheses (flagship examples: $L_{\chi_4}$ at $\delta = 1/2$, and $p \equiv 1 \pmod 5$ at $\delta = 1/4$), every finite nonzero binary word $w$ of length $k$ occurs $\Theta_{w,a}(H / (\log N)^{1-\delta})$ times in every moving window $[N, N + H)$ with $N^a \le H \le N$ ($7/12 < a \le 1$).
  - Every such $L_f$ is base-2 disjunctive, irrational, non-normal with one-digit frequency $O((\log N)^{-(1-\delta)})$, and satisfies the exact two-sided discrepancy gap
    ```math
    1 - D_N(L_f) \asymp \frac{\log\log N}{(\log N)^{1-\delta}}.
    ```
  - For every odd Dirichlet character $\chi$ (primitive or imprimitive), $L_\chi$ is transcendental over $\mathbb{Q}$ via weight-1 modular forms (Hamieh–Murty 2016, Thms 1.1 & 3.1).
- **Dependencies:** Shiu (1980) Theorem 1, Wirsing (1961) Satz 1, Perelli–Pintz–Salerno (*Invent. Math.* **79**, 1985, 1–9, $Q = x^{1/40}$), Selberg sieve, Montgomery–Vaughan Brun–Titchmarsh, Hamieh–Murty (2016); kernel-verified discrepancy upper/lower transfers in `Phase17DiscrepancyBounds.lean` and arrival identities in `WeightedLambertIdentities.lean` / `ErdosBorweinArrivals.lean`.
- **Prior-art comparison needed:** Check recent literature on disjunctive non-normal Lambert series (beyond Erdős–Borwein disjunctivity by Captain Sude / Mira Cult and Mahler–Bugeaud constructions) and verify that the exact $(\log\log N)/(\log N)^{1-\delta}$ discrepancy gap for $L_\chi$ is new.
- **External review focus:** Analytic number theory referee to double-check the Perelli–Pintz–Salerno uniform short-interval Bombieri–Vinogradov application, the Selberg sieve $G(z)$ lower bound, and the left-extension separated-mass lower bound.

### 2. Revised Paper I: Polynomial images of Stoneham numbers
- **Headline theorems:** Full coprime-$c > 2$ polynomial normality & joint uniformity (Lean), quantitative $O(N^{-\delta_d})$ discrepancy rates, plus at $c = 2$: exact least orbit order $F(d) = \sum_{k \le d} \lfloor \log_2 k \rfloor$ with uniqueness of the two minimizers, $M_d \neq 0$ on $J_d$ for all $d$, $p$-adic cyclic descent mod $p^k$, normality for $3 \le d \le 120$, $d = 128$, and 16 infinite families $e p^r$, exact quadratic plateau spectra (including unconditional bounds $< 1/4$ such as $\frac{1}{4}(1 - \frac{1}{3 \cdot 2^{v_2(b+1)}})$), and echelon-degree joint normality.
- **Dependencies:** `PeriodicPolynomialNormal.lean`, `QuantitativeStoneham*.lean`, `Phase16StonehamBounds.lean`, `Phase17DiscrepancyBounds.lean`, Vandehey (preprint Theorem 5.1) for quantitative rates, and exact finite certificates in `scripts/phase16_dyadic_stoneham.py` / `scripts/phase17_dyadic_stoneham.py`.
- **External review focus:** Diophantine/normal-numbers specialist to compare with Bailey–Crandall (2002), Coons (2013), Vandehey, and prior Stoneham-polynomial literature, and check whether the $c = 2$ section should remain in Paper I or split into a companion paper.

### 3. Exceptional orbits of independent-prime Stoneham products (New Standalone Paper)
- **Headline theorems:**
  - Exact empirical-mixture limit theorem $D^+(x) = \max_{w \in \mathcal{W}} \Delta(\sigma(w))$ and non-additivity of binary $3^r 5^s$ cycle mixtures.
  - Exact zero-slack equivalence $D^+(\alpha(2,3)\alpha(2,5)) \le 4/11 \iff \limsup \#\{n < N : R_n \in \mathbb{Z}\}/N \le 4/11$, sharpening Phase 16's unconditional bound $4/11 + 2.85 \times 10^{-10}$.
  - Eisenstein proof that $\zeta_{p,a,d} \notin \mathbb{Z}$ for all nonrectangular shapes.
  - Infinite-family generalization: for every distinct odd prime pair $(p, q)$, infinitely many radices $B \ge 2$ satisfy logarithmic equidistribution, $D^- = 0$, power-tower shell spacing, $O_\varepsilon(\log^* T)$ scale sparsity, and $D^+ \le M_{p,q} + \varepsilon$ where $M_{p,q} = (p-1)(q-1)/(2pq - p - q)$, certified for $(2,3,7)$, $(2,3,13)$, and $(36,5,7)$.
- **Dependencies:** `ProductThreeFive*.lean`, `Phase16StonehamBounds.lean`, `Phase17DiscrepancyBounds.lean`, and `scripts/phase17_mixed_product_orbits.py`.
- **External review focus:** $p$-adic / Diophantine specialist to audit the Mahler–Newton ultrametric spacing, the Eisenstein non-integrality argument, and the zero-slack $4/11$ reduction.

### 4. Updated Paper II: Localized logarithms and polylogarithms
- **Headline theorems:** Complete zero-or-one discrepancy dichotomy on finite prime supports (LS-01), full polynomial-weight classification (MW-01), and kernel-verified joint uniformity across all distinct positive orders (`LocalizedJoint.lean`) with computable positive-tolerance common depth.
- **External review focus:** Normal-numbers / $p$-adic analysis referee to compare with earlier localized-logarithm results on singleton axes.

### 5. Expanded Paper V: Classical constant denominators and the window–denominator barrier
- **Headline theorems:** Universal balanced completeness ($5/12$ floor + $(5/12, 13/30)$ gap across all balanced algebraic trace sources, adaptive height bounds up to $N/528$ backed by a 730-node Lean branch-and-bound certificate), Chebyshev $D^{-2}$ analytic floor vs. Legendre–Bezout linear windows ($T_N = \log_2 L_D - O(1)$ with $w = 0$), exact identification of Legendre–Bezout numerators with $\operatorname{round}(L_D 2^N \ln 2)$, unconditional window–denominator barrier $T_N \le (\mu - 1)N + \mu \log_2 q_N + O(1)$ excluding incomplete-orbit normality transfer ($T_N \ge q_N^\delta$) at any positive denominator rate, adaptive coset ceiling, and unified comparison of $\ln 2, \pi, e, \sqrt{2}, \varphi$.
- **External review focus:** Diophantine approximation / Padé / Beukers–Rukhadze–Marcovecchio specialist.

### 6. Expanded Paper IV: Arithmetic certificates, limitations, and computable undecidability
- **Headline theorems:** Smooth-orbit certificates (AC-01), partial coverage (AC-02), overlap rigidity (AC-03), counting obstruction (AC-10), unified 8-class obstruction taxonomy with paired constructions, kernel-verified halting reductions over arbitrary digit-oracle codes (`ComputableNormalityUndecidability.lean`), and consistency-only Rosser independence in PA/ZFC (explicitly noting no independence conclusion for $\ln 2, \pi, e$).
- **Prior-art positioning:** Must explicitly cite Ki–Linton (1994), Becher–Heiber–Slaman (2014, 2015), Becher–Slaman (2014), and Lutz–Mayordomo (2016) so the scope is framed accurately as an elementary formalizable two-limit promise reduction rather than a priority claim on Borel/effective completeness.

### 7. Collaborative Manuscript: Erdős–Borwein statistical thresholds
- **Headline theorems:** Sharp 16-column threshold ($\Psi_{16}$ sufficient from Phase 14's tail mean; exact countermodels for $D \le 15$), certified $p_* \in [0.492450290, 0.492460864]$, exact column-hierarchy countermodels ($k \le 4$), parity Bonferroni theorem ($r \ge 5$ globally, $9 \le r_{\min}(16) \le 92$; kernel-verified dual inequality in `Phase17DiscrepancyBounds.lean`), trigonometric degree threshold ($116$ certified via Selberg–Vaaler; $\le 110$ ruled out by explicit dual measure), Crandall (2012) audit, and explicit arithmetic correlation targets.
- **Next collaborative steps:** (i) Independent segmented-sieve computation of certified binary shell frequencies to $s \approx 30$ to settle the Crandall $2^{43}$ drift question; (ii) upper-bound sieve analogue of Mira Cult's framework on $\Psi_{16}$; (iii) almost-all-scales $r$-point correlations of $e(\theta V)\chi(U)$ beyond Tao–Teräväinen ($r = 2$).
