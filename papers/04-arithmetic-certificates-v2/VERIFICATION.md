## What is completely kernel-verified

At the pinned baseline commit [`e4809cf561573aef588e6ffc491a35cdb79fd9ec`](https://github.com/eldernos/MathematicsResearch/tree/e4809cf561573aef588e6ffc491a35cdb79fd9ec) (Lean 4.36.0; full build, `Audit.lean`, and trust-level-0 kernel replay recorded in [`notes/phase18/verification.md`](https://github.com/eldernos/MathematicsResearch/blob/e4809cf561573aef588e6ffc491a35cdb79fd9ec/notes/phase18/verification.md), checking 1,593 public declarations and all 248 modules with only the three standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`):

3. **Theorem 3.1 (AC-03):** Complete proof of uniform separation of noncongruent smooth rational models on a shared segment (`smooth_overlap_separation`). Both halves of Corollary 3.2 (`smooth_overlap_rigidity`, `overlap_chain_denominator`) are individually kernel-verified.

5. **Supporting finite inputs:** Radix-orbit specializations of Proposition 2.1 (`QualityCoverage.lean`, `PartialUniformity.lean`) and the weighted word-counting inequality of Theorem 4.1 (`few_symbol_words` in `ModelCounting.lean`).

## External analytic dependencies

- **Weyl (1916), §1 (pp. 314–318):** Weyl's equidistribution criterion (used in the written proof of Theorem 1.1; also formalized independently inside `NormalityChecker/Weyl.lean`).
- **First Borel–Cantelli lemma:** Standard measure theory (used in Theorem 4.1).
- **Hadamard (1896), pp. 199–220:** Prime number theorem in the form $\sum_{p \le y} \log p \sim y$ (used in Section 6, Item 3).
- **Furstenberg (1967), Theorem IV.1 (p. 41):** Topological density of $\{2^u 3^v x \bmod 1\}$ for irrational $x$ (used in Section 6, Item 6).
- **van der Poorten and Shallit (1992), pp. 240–242:** Bounded partial quotients of the folded continued fraction for $2\sum_{j \ge 0} 2^{-2^j}$ (used in Section 6, Item 8).
- **Rosser (1936), Theorem II (pp. 87–91):** Consistency-only Rosser fixed point and unprovability argument in PA/ZFC (used in Theorem 8.1).

Published results cited purely for comparison and positioning (not used as proof steps): Korobov (1972), Bailey–Crandall (2002), Vandehey (2019), Wagner (1995), Schmidt (1960), Ki–Linton (1994), Becher–Heiber–Slaman (2014, 2015), Becher–Slaman (2014), Rice (1953), Specker (1949), Sierpiński (1917), Becher–Figueira (2002), Becher–Figueira–Picchi (2007), Lutz–Mayordomo (2016/2021), Captain Sude (2026), and Mira Cult (2026).
