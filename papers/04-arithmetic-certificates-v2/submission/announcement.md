## Mathematics forum announcement (one paragraph)

This draft announcement accompanies version 2.0 of the preprint draft *Arithmetic Certificates for Normality and Mathematical Limitations* (`papers/04-arithmetic-certificates-v2/`), superseding version 1.1. The paper pairs a block-level arithmetic certificate framework for base-$b$ normality (using a self-contained Lean 4 proof of a qualitative form of Vandehey's fixed-support exponential-sum estimate) and partial-coverage discrepancy bounds with deterministic, measure-theoretic, method-specific, computability, and proof-theoretic limitations: uniform overlap rigidity for smooth rational models, a counting obstruction (Theorem 4.1) showing that polynomial-denominator rational models meeting the mean circle error threshold $1/(16b^4)$ on an independently prescribed geometric schedule with polynomially many starts per scale occur only finitely often for almost every real, explicit computable binary pairs separating odd-primary rational data and logarithmic word laws from ordinary normality, eight concrete barriers for specific proof methods, uniform halting reductions on arbitrary digit-oracle codes (`Nat.Partrec.Code`) under the transcendental $\{0,1\}$-discrepancy promise (whose individual component declarations are Lean 4 kernel-verified under explicit endpoint hypotheses, and which we distinguish from the boldface Borel $\boldsymbol{\Pi}^0_3$-completeness theorem of Ki–Linton and the effective/boldface $\Pi^0_3$-completeness theorems of Becher–Heiber–Slaman on unrestricted reals and indices), and a separate written consistency-relative Rosser independence construction for theory-dependent digit programs in PA and ZFC (establishing neither normality nor independence for any named classical constant). The project is AI-assisted; the Lean 4 kernel checked the declarations listed in `VERIFICATION.md` (including only the finite weighted word inequality for Theorem 4.1), while the written proofs and literature comparisons have undergone only cross-model AI review and await independent human specialist review.
