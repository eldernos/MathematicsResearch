## Mathematics forum announcement (one paragraph)

We have posted version 2.0 of the preprint draft *Arithmetic Certificates for Normality and Mathematical Limitations* (`papers/04-arithmetic-certificates-v2/`), superseding version 1.1. The paper pairs a block-level arithmetic certificate framework for base-$b$ normality (using a self-contained Lean 4 proof of a qualitative form of Vandehey's fixed-support exponential-sum estimate) and partial-coverage discrepancy bounds with deterministic, measure-theoretic, method-specific, computability, and proof-theoretic limitations: uniform overlap rigidity for smooth rational models, a counting obstruction showing that prescribed-schedule polynomial-denominator approximations occur only finitely often for almost every real, explicit computable binary pairs separating odd-primary rational data and logarithmic word laws from ordinary normality, eight concrete barriers for specific proof methods, kernel-verified Lean 4 halting reductions on arbitrary digit-oracle codes (`Nat.Partrec.Code`) under the transcendental $\{0,1\}$-discrepancy promise (positioned alongside the stronger $\Pi^0_3$-completeness theorems of Ki–Linton and Becher–Heiber–Slaman), and a separate written consistency-relative Rosser independence construction for theory-dependent digit programs in PA and ZFC (with no implication for any named classical constant). The project is AI-assisted; the Lean 4 kernel checked the declarations listed in `VERIFICATION.md`, while the written proofs and literature comparisons have undergone only cross-model AI review and await independent human specialist review.
