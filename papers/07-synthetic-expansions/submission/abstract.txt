Let $b \ge 2$ be an integer base, $p$ a prime not dividing $b$, and $t$ the order of $b$ modulo $p$. For an integer $d \ge 2$, insert the coefficient $p^{-s v_p(k)}$ at base-$b$ position $t k^d$ for every $k \ge 1$. For $s = 0$ the extreme discrepancy tends to one; for every integer $s \ge 1$ the number is normal to base $b$, on every strictly increasing schedule of multiples of $t$ dominating $k^2$. This switch and the exact reduced denominator of every shifted prefix are kernel-checked in Lean 4. For odd $p$ and $d \ge s+2$ complete periods give an explicit power rate; for $\xi = \sum_k 3^{-v_3(k)} 2^{-2k^3}$ a short proof gives $D_N \le 200 N^{-1/3}$. Every radix orbit is, after a finite shift, the orbit of a positive expansion of this kind, so prescribed discrepancy limits and limit sets exist by classical realization. Choosing the state after each cubic insertion from two explicit targets, with denominators at most $pk$, attains every limiting discrepancy in $[0,1]$ with error $O(N^{-1/3})$, uniformly. For odd $p$, $s \ge 1$ and an integer $d \ge 3$, every nonconstant rational polynomial of total degree at most two in members of this family with distinct orders on a common schedule is normal; in particular $\xi^2$ is normal and $(\xi, \xi^2)$ is jointly normal. Finally a positive binary series using every odd prime as a denominator prime is normal and Liouville with $D_N = O(1/\log N)$.
