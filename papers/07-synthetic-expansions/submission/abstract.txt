Let $b \ge 2$ be an integer base, $p$ a prime not dividing $b$, and $t$ the order of $b$ modulo $p$. Insert the coefficient $p^{-s v_p(k)}$ at base-$b$ position $t k^d$ for every $k \ge 1$. For $s = 0$ the extreme discrepancy tends to one; for every integer $s \ge 1$ the number is normal to base $b$, on every strictly increasing schedule of multiples of $t$ dominating $k^2$. This switch and the exact reduced denominator of every shifted prefix are kernel-checked in Lean 4. For odd $p$ and $d \ge s+2$ complete periods give an explicit power rate; for $\xi = \sum_k 3^{-v_3(k)} 2^{-2k^3}$ a short proof gives $D_N \le 200 N^{-1/3}$. Positive rational state corrections on a cubic schedule realize every limiting discrepancy in $[0,1]$ with error $O(N^{-1/3})$, every pair of lower and upper limits, and every nonempty compact connected set of invariant measures as a limit set. For odd $p$, $s \ge 1$ and $d \ge 3$, every nonconstant rational polynomial of total degree at most two in members of this family with distinct orders on a common schedule is normal; in particular $\xi^2$ is normal and $(\xi, \xi^2)$ is jointly normal. The proof shows that the limiting $p$-adic mass of the shifted prefixes has Lebesgue-null level sets. Finally a positive binary series using every odd prime as a denominator prime is normal and Liouville with $D_N = O(1/\log N)$.
