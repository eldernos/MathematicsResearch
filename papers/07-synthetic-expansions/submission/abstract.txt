Let $b \ge 2$ be an integer base, $p$ a prime not dividing $b$, and $t$ the order of $b$ modulo $p$. Insert the coefficient $p^{-s v_p(k)}$ at base-$b$ position $t k^d$ for every $k \ge 1$. For $s = 0$ the extreme discrepancy tends to one; for every integer $s \ge 1$ the number is normal to base $b$. This switch holds on every strictly increasing schedule of multiples of $t$ dominating $k^2$, and is kernel-checked in Lean 4 together with the exact reduced denominator of every shifted prefix. For odd $p$ and $d \ge s+2$, complete periods give an explicit power rate; for the binary number $\sum_k 3^{-v_3(k)} 2^{-2k^3}$ a short self-contained proof gives $D_N \le 200 N^{-1/3}$. Choosing the rational state after each cubic insertion by a positive correction realizes every limiting discrepancy in $[0,1]$ with error $O(N^{-1/3})$, every pair of lower and upper limits, and every nonempty compact connected set of invariant measures as a limit set. We prove the finite identity $\sup_x |D_N(x+z) - D_N(x)| = 1 - D_N(z)$, characterize the translations that preserve discrepancy for every input, and show that the limits of $D_N(x)$, $D_N(y)$, $D_N(x+y)$ are exactly the triples with $\max(0, \alpha+\beta-1) \le \gamma \le 1 - |\alpha-\beta|$, all realized at rate $O(N^{-1/3})$. A positive binary series using every odd prime as a denominator prime is normal and Liouville with $D_N = O(1/\log N)$, while positive rational multiples of its jointly normal components can sum to any positive rational.
