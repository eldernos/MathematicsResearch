## Paragraph for a mathematics forum

This preprint draft studies sparse base-$b$ expansions whose rational part after each shift is controlled exactly. If the coefficient $p^{-sv_p(k)}$ is inserted at position $tk^d$, where $t$ is the order of $b$ modulo $p$, the number is normal for every $s\ge1$, while its discrepancy tends to one for $s=0$; this switch is checked in Lean 4. For $\xi=\sum_k3^{-v_3(k)}2^{-2k^3}$ a two-page proof gives $D_N\le200N^{-1/3}$, and a longer analytic argument shows that $\xi^2$ is normal and $(\xi,\xi^2)$ jointly normal: the leading $3$-adic mass of the shifted prefixes of $\xi^2$ has null level sets, proved with van der Corput's method and a second-moment argument. More generally every rational polynomial of total degree two in such numbers of distinct orders is normal. The same denominator mechanism prescribes any limiting discrepancy, any pair of lower and upper limits, and any compact connected set of invariant measures, and gives a normal Liouville binary series using every odd prime as a denominator prime. Several ingredients have classical precedents (Korobov, Stoneham, Kano, Kano–Shiokawa, Wagner, Colebrook, Sigmund), which the paper cites. The written proofs have been checked only by AI models, and comments from specialists are very welcome.
