## Paragraph for a mathematics forum

This preprint draft studies sparse base-$b$ expansions whose rational part after each shift is chosen in advance. If the coefficient $p^{-sv_p(k)}$ is inserted at position $tk^d$, where $t$ is the order of $b$ modulo $p$, the number is normal for every $s\ge1$, while its discrepancy tends to one for $s=0$. This switch is checked in Lean 4. For $\sum_k3^{-v_3(k)}2^{-2k^3}$ a two-page proof gives $D_N\le200N^{-1/3}$.

The same mechanism prescribes any limiting discrepancy, any pair of lower and upper limits, and any compact connected set of invariant measures as a limit set. The paper also proves the identity $\sup_x|D_N(x+z)-D_N(x)|=1-D_N(z)$, determines the possible limit triples for $x$, $y$ and $x+y$, and gives a normal Liouville binary series that uses every odd prime as a denominator prime.

Several ingredients have classical precedents, which the paper cites: Korobov, Stoneham, Kano, Colebrook and Sigmund, Rauzy, and Bergelson–Downarowicz. The rates are elementary and far from the best known for other constructions. The written proofs have been checked only by AI models, and comments from specialists are very welcome.
