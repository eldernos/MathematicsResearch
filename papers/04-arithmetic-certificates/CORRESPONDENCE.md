## Paper deductions and finite computation

The shortest-path optimality assertion after Proposition 4 concerns scalar data subject to the given edge bounds. Section 4 proves the two-prime logarithmic density and the lower bound it gives for upper ordinary density. The clipped piecewise-affine optimization in the binary example uses exact rational arithmetic. The source data contain every crossing used for both cutoffs. [Figure calculation](figures/joint_density.py) · [Rational vertices](figures/joint_density.csv)

The finite word inequality supplies the exponential tail in Theorem 8 after normalization by the number of equiprobable digit words. The count over rational residues, summation over starts and first Borel–Cantelli argument are paper deductions. The counterexamples in Section 5 are direct elementary calculations.
