The text below describes the five archived v1.1 manuscripts and is kept as the historical record.

These five manuscripts develop results in the formal library of *Normality and Discrepancy of Arithmetic Expansions*. Papers I and II are the principal mathematical papers. Paper III treats signed expansions. Paper IV supplies the methods, and Paper V records what these methods currently prove for classical constants. The original verified research baseline is [Checkpoint 11](https://github.com/eldernos/MathematicsResearch/commit/a09e011720bc74396a27f4cb3ce2d8a5b3ce5035). The [result registry](../docs/results.md) distinguishes kernel proofs, paper deductions and finite computations.

## I. Polynomial images of Stoneham numbers with an odd prime-power parameter

For a radix *b* coprime to an odd prime *p*, every nonconstant rational polynomial in the standard Stoneham number with parameter *p*ᵉ is normal in base *b*. The paper proves uniform survival of the leading denominator on the insertion scales, then applies a fixed-support orbit estimate. It includes the binary-normal square’s exact denominator profile, the paper proof of transcendence and strict upper discrepancy for independent-parameter products, and joint uniformity of the radix orbits of distinct positive powers. Registry ST-01 through ST-06. [PDF](01-stoneham-polynomials/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](01-stoneham-polynomials/paper.tex) · [Reviewer guide](01-stoneham-polynomials/REVIEW.md)

## II. Localized logarithms and polylogarithms

On the full support generated by a finite set of denominator primes, the unsigned expansion of every positive order is normal if some support prime fails to divide the radix. When every support prime divides the radix, its interval discrepancy tends to one. The proof studies deep prime cancellation on logarithmic scales. The polynomial-weight classification also implies joint uniformity at distinct positive orders on one fixed support containing an off-radix prime. This corollary is a paper deduction. Registry LS-01 through LS-03 and MW-01. [PDF](02-localized-normality/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](02-localized-normality/paper.tex) · [Reviewer guide](02-localized-normality/REVIEW.md)

## III. Resonance and discrepancy in signed expansions

Cyclotomic conditions determine when cancellation in finite Stoneham combinations produces nonnormal sums. For a family with signed coefficients, the discrepancy along each prefix tracks the proportion of positions in zero-phase shells. Periodic values, rational translations, and the attainable spectrum follow from this description. Registry CR-01 and DS-01 through DS-04. [PDF](03-signed-resonance/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](03-signed-resonance/paper.tex) · [Reviewer guide](03-signed-resonance/REVIEW.md)

## IV. Arithmetic certificates for normality

This technical report relates short modular orbits to radix digit counts. It specifies the assumptions on prime support, approximation errors, denominators and block lengths, and derives discrepancy bounds from partial or overlapping coverage. Separation and counting results explain when a proposed family of rational models cannot certify a desired fraction of the orbit. Registry AC-01 through AC-03. [PDF](04-arithmetic-certificates/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](04-arithmetic-certificates/paper.tex) · [Reviewer guide](04-arithmetic-certificates/REVIEW.md)

## V. Reduced denominators of binary logarithm and arctangent series

A two-stride representation of ln 2 has reduced-denominator growth rate one half. The paper separates this exact rate from upper budgets for corrected approximations and from finite exclusions of low-transition binary words. It also treats related models of π, *e*, and rational arctangents. The strict asymptotic discrepancy target for ln 2 remains open. Registry CC-01 and CC-02. [PDF](05-classical-denominators/paper.pdf) · [LaTeX](05-classical-denominators/paper.tex) · [Reviewer guide](05-classical-denominators/REVIEW.md)

## Circulation priorities

Papers I and II are the first candidates for review by a mathematician. In Paper I the contribution to assess is the theorem for the standard odd-prime-power Stoneham generators, including independently chosen strides and radix dilations. Prior normal-ring constructions and the literature on simultaneous normality already cover related questions. In Paper II the comparison is with earlier localized-logarithm and singleton-axis results, with particular attention to mixed supports and all positive orders. The reviewer guides give the precise source comparisons.

Paper III groups the cyclotomic and signed-shell results in one manuscript and treats the positive normal-summand construction in an appendix. Paper IV is a companion technical report on the methods and their limitations. Paper V is a research draft on denominator arithmetic, with lower submission priority. Historical priority and any personal byline require the owner's and specialists' judgment.
