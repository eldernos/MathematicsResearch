## Paragraph for a mathematics forum

The preprint "Resonance and discrepancy in signed Stoneham expansions" (version 1.2, a named-author edition of version 1.1 with unchanged mathematics) studies sparse radix expansions with bounded integer phases inserted at powers of a prime *p* coprime to the radix. At every prefix, the interval discrepancy equals the proportion of positions in zero-phase shells up to an error tending to zero. The attainable upper discrepancies are exactly the base-*p* values, with digits 0 and *p* − 1, of the suffix-maximal binary words studied by Kaneko and Steiner. Rational translation multiplies the main term by the discrepancy of a finite orbit, which gives transcendental examples of arbitrarily small positive discrepancy. For finite rational combinations of dilated components, normality is decided by a cyclotomic divisibility test. The principal theorems are verified in Lean 4. The written proofs and the literature comparisons have been checked only by AI models, not by a human expert. The paper was produced in an AI-assisted project, as disclosed in the manuscript. Corrections are welcome in the repository issue tracker.
