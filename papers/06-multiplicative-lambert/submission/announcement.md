## Mathematics forum

We have posted a preprint draft on the binary digits of Lambert series Σ f(m)/(2ᵐ−1) whose coefficients c = f∗1 are multiplicative and supported on primes of density δ < 1, for example c = r₂(n)/4 (δ = 1/2) or c supported on primes ≡ 1 mod 5 (δ = 1/4). For a > 7/12 we prove that every fixed word containing a one occurs between constant multiples of H/(log N)^{1−δ} times in each window [N, N+H) with N^a ≤ H ≤ N. The number is therefore disjunctive but not simply normal, and the discrepancy gap 1 − D_N has exact order (log log N)/(log N)^{1−δ}. The lower bounds plant a single coefficient c(bp) at the midpoint of the target cylinder and use a Selberg sieve to make neighbouring coefficients vanish, following the planted-prime approach developed for the Erdős–Borwein constant by Campbell, Captain Sude and Mira Cult. For odd characters, transcendence follows from Hamieh–Murty. Lean 4 checks the finite discrepancy inequalities only. The analytic estimates are written proofs that have been checked by AI models but not yet by a human specialist, and we would welcome corrections, especially on the sieve step and on the short-interval prime input behind the condition a > 7/12.
