# Announcement drafts

## Short post (at most 280 characters)

Preprint: for S a finite prime set, sums of m^-s b^-m over m with prime factors in S have discrepancy -> 0 iff some prime of S doesn't divide b (else 1). Builds on Sude, Mira, Mira Cult for supports prime to b or two generators. Lean-checked; no human review.

## Paragraph for a mathematics forum

The preprint "Normality of localized logarithms and polylogarithms on finite prime supports" (version 2.0) studies the series sum of m^{-s} b^{-m} over the positive integers m whose prime factors lie in a fixed finite set S, for a radix b >= 2. For every order s >= 1 the base-b interval discrepancy tends to 0 if some prime of S does not divide b and to 1 otherwise, and for a nonzero rational polynomial numerator the series over all of M(S) is normal exactly when the weight has a pole at zero and such a prime exists. Public work of Captain Sude and of Mira and Mira Cult already covers order one for supports prime to the radix, binary supports containing 3 and two generators, and all orders for supports prime to the radix, and the paper records that these arguments extend further, for example to all orders when the least prime of S is off the radix; the paper's contribution is the remaining mixed supports, with the polynomial, joint and retained-subset statements on multi-prime supports. Persistent cancellation of denominators at an off-radix prime is shown to have measure zero by a recurrence argument on a logarithmic circle combined with p-adic separation of finitely many geometric rays. If some prime of S does not divide b, the shifts at distinct positive orders on one fixed support are jointly equidistributed, and for a prime p of S not dividing b, a numerator with a pole at zero and a retained subset of the support, the discrepancy is bounded by the missed density of the windows [p^k, 2p^k) of retained powers of p. The main classifications and the joint theorem are verified in Lean 4; the written proofs, the literature comparisons (including the overlap with public notes of Mira, Mira Cult and Captain Sude, and with work of Stoneham, Bailey-Crandall, Kano and Vandehey) and the priority statements have been checked only by AI models, not by a human expert. The paper was produced in an AI-assisted project, as disclosed in the manuscript. Comments and corrections are welcome in the repository issue tracker.
