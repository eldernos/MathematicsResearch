## Short post (at most 280 characters)

New preprint: sums of m^-s b^-m over integers whose prime factors lie in a finite set S are normal in base b iff some prime of S doesn't divide b (discrepancy -> 0 or 1). Includes joint equidistribution at distinct orders. Lean-checked; no human review yet.

## Paragraph for a mathematics forum

The preprint "Normality of localized logarithms and polylogarithms on finite prime supports" (version 2.0) studies the series sum of m^{-s} b^{-m} over the positive integers m whose prime factors lie in a fixed finite set S. For every order s >= 1 the base-b interval discrepancy tends to 0 if some prime of S does not divide b and to 1 otherwise, and for rational polynomial numerators the series is normal exactly when the weight has a pole at zero and such a prime exists. Persistent cancellation of denominators at an off-radix prime is shown to have measure zero by a recurrence argument on a logarithmic circle combined with p-adic separation of finitely many geometric rays. The shifts at distinct positive orders on one fixed support are jointly equidistributed, and for retained subsets of the support the discrepancy is bounded by the missed density of prime-power windows. The main classifications and the joint theorem are verified in Lean 4; the written proofs, the literature comparisons (including overlap with public notes of Mira, Mira Cult and Captain Sude, and with work of Stoneham, Bailey-Crandall and Vandehey) and the priority statements have been checked only by AI models, not by a human expert. The paper was produced in an AI-assisted project, as disclosed in the manuscript. Comments and corrections are welcome in the repository issue tracker.
