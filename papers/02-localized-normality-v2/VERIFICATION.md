## What is completely kernel-verified

Each item is one pinned declaration (or one `Check.lean` theorem built from pinned declarations).

- **Theorem A**: the mathlib-only statement `PaperII.UnsignedClassification` in `Statements.lean` (limit statement and normality for J nonempty) is proved in `Check.lean` as `PaperII.unsignedClassification` from `unsigned_discrepancy_limit` and `unsigned_finite_support_normal`. Its definitions (smooth indices `m = i+1`, half-open frequencies `visits`, `Normal`, `discrepancy`) use only mathlib; the empirical definitions agree with the library's by unfolding, and the zero summand of the polynomial library is removed in `polynomialValue_eq`. The sentence "normal exactly when J is nonempty" is the case P = 1 of `PaperII.polynomialClassification`; the limit 1 for J empty is `unsigned_discrepancy_limit`.
- **Theorem B(i)**, the equivalence (`PaperII.polynomialClassification`, including order `s = 0`). **Theorem B(ii)** (`rational_boundary`, which quantifies over the mask before giving an integer `L` coprime to `b`) and **B(iii)** (`integer_mask_maximal`) are Lean statements in the library's definitions (`maskedValue`, `radixDiscrepancy`, `NormalIn`); they are *not* restated in mathlib-only form in `Statements.lean`. The identification `L = D_0` (the part of a common denominator of the coefficients prime to `b`) is a written argument, and not part of the kernel statement.
- **Theorem C(i)** for rational numerators `P_i(0) != 0`: `PaperII.jointPolynomial` in `Check.lean` (mathlib-only statement). The unsigned case is also `LocalizedJointStatements.DistinctOrders`, proved as `distinct_orders_checked`.
- **Theorem C(ii),(iii)**, **Theorem D(ii)** (the bounded-gap display, `rational_pole_bound`), **Theorem F**, **Theorem 4.1**, **Theorem 11.1** (one declaration per assertion), **Proposition 6.3** and **Example 5.1**: Lean statements in the library's own definitions. `maskedValue b S keep P t` is `series b (weights ...)` with `weights S keep P t m = P(m)/m^t` on retained smooth `m` (including the vanishing `m = 0` term) and `JointUniform` is the box-frequency statement over positions `0..N-1` of `Int.fract (b^n * x_i)`. - **Example 11.2**: the four facts listed in its row of the table.
- Axiom audit: `AuditDeclarations.lean` checks 42 declarations; all depend only on `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, and the file now raises an error (non-zero exit status) on a missing declaration or an unexpected axiom, so that its exit status is audit evidence (an earlier version printed BAD or MISSING and exited successfully). `Check.lean` does the same for its three theorems.

## External analytic dependencies

- Vandehey, *Differencing methods for Korobov-type exponential sums*, J. Anal. Math. 138 (2019), 405-439, Corollary 1.2 (numbering and pages of the author manuscript arXiv:1606.07911v1, pp. 2-3, checked on 25 November 2026; the version of record was not accessible): for a fixed finite prime set `P`, `m` supported on `P`, `gcd(a,m) = gcd(m,b) = 1`, `N >= m^epsilon`, the sum `|sum_{n<=N} e(a b^n / m)|` is at most `C N m^(-delta) (1 + log m)`. Used in Lemma 2.1 only. The library contains an independent proof (`fixed_support_orbit_estimate`, `SmoothOrbit.lean#L207`), so none of the kernel-verified theorems depends on the citation.
- Weyl's criterion (one and several variables), Poincare recurrence for a rotation, and elementary facts on eventual periodicity in a finite monoid appear in written proofs only as textbook inputs.

## Reproduce

The files `Statements.lean`, `Check.lean`, `AuditDeclarations.lean`, `check.sh` and `check_order_two.py` of this directory are **not** in the checkpoint `e4809cf`. ```sh

```sh
  NormalityChecker.MaskPeriods NormalityChecker.MaskDensity NormalityChecker.MaskQuotients NormalityChecker.MaskThirds \

papers/02-localized-normality-v2/check.sh

wsl -d Ubuntu -- bash papers/wsl-build.sh 02-localized-normality-v2
```
