They independently obtained the order-two denominator 7 and found no counterexample to the principal classifications. [Gemini report](gemini.md) · [Sonnet report](sonnet.md)

| Finding | Change in the release manuscript |
| --- | --- |
| Asymptotic and exact claims in Appendix A | The abstract specifies an additive error tending to zero at each cutoff. The appendix now introduces its proof as independent of the individual nullity theorem and explains what the latter implies for the limiting score. The compact maximum is an exact statement about the upper limit. |
| Full support, restrictions and the formal contracts | The text and correspondence table distinguish the two independently restated classifications from the separately verified grid, restricted-support, cancellation and 2/3 results. Each proof entry links to its declaration. The paper pins both the library revision and the later statement/check files. |
| Transfer and examples | The block bound now gives explicit uniform quantifiers and invokes the qualitative fixed-support estimate. The rational prefix has its summation limits on the sum itself. The dyadic 2/3 example includes the alternating numerator calculation. The quotient-weight argument states the hypotheses needed for transfer. |
| Antecedents and termination | The text cites Stoneham in the singleton case, explains the earlier quantitative binary rate, and uses a narrower claim about Vandehey. The ancillary Subspace Theorem result was removed from the article's argument; it is unnecessary for convergence of the certificate bounds. The pinned 2026 source is identified as a repository manuscript. |
| Exposition, figure and reproducibility | The empty set uses the same notation in the figure and the text. Technical vocabulary used only to describe internal workflow was removed from the mathematical sections. Build commands are in the reviewer guide and the AI disclosure describes the assistance directly. |
