## 3. Detailed Findings (Ordered by Severity)

### Major Findings

#### [M1] Appendix A does not frame Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.3 as nullity-independent, making them appear vacuous downstream of Theorem 4.1
- **Location:** Appendix A, pp. 8–10 (`paper.tex:215–281`).
- **Evidence:** By Section 4, the reader already knows Theorem 4.1 ($\vol(Z_{p,s}) = 0$ for every $p \in J$) and Theorem 1.1 ($D_N(X_s) \to 0$ when $J \ne \varnothing$, $D_N(X_s) \to 1$ when $J = \varnothing$). Since $B_p = \bigcap_{d=1}^s Z_{p,d} \subseteq Z_{p,s}$, Theorem 4.1 immediately implies that whenever $J \ne \varnothing$:
  - $\vol(B_p) = 0$, hence $\vol(B_* \cap [1, N)) = 0$ for every $N$;
  - $F_L(N)/N \le \#\{1 \le n < N : t_p(n) \in E_{p,s,L}\}/N \le C_{p,s,L} + o(1) \to 0$;
  - $W_p$ is null, so the integrand in Corollary A.3 is $0$ for every $\theta \in H$.
  And when $J = \varnothing$, $B_* = [1, \infty)$ so both sides are identically $1$. Thus **every conclusion of Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.3 follows in three lines from Theorems 1.1 and 4.1** as stated. Only in the penultimate sentence of Appendix A (p. 10, `paper.tex:281`) does the text remark: *"For these unsigned series Theorem 4.1 evaluates the maximum completely."*
- **Impact:** A mathematician refereeing the paper will question why 2.5 pages are devoted to proving identities whose sides are already known to be identically $0$ or $1$. The real value of Appendix A is that (i) Lemma A.2 and the $n = 186$ counterexample explain the *local arithmetic clearing obstruction* at order $s \ge 2$, and (ii) Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.3 are proved **without invoking Theorem 4.1**, showing structurally how prefix discrepancy tracks simultaneous persistent failures across all orders and scales.
- **Recommendation:** State explicitly in the opening paragraph of Appendix A that Theorem A.1 and Corollary A.3 are proved *independently of Theorem 4.1* (and clarify whether the same method is intended as a structural template for general weighted/signed moments where persistent zeros can have positive measure). Also note why the $s=1$ single-prime specialization in Corollary A.3 (p. 9, `paper.tex:274–275`) holds for *every* $s \ge 1$ with $B_p$ in place of $Z_{p,1}$—restricting it to $s=1$ is an artifact of an earlier Lean declaration (`PersistentRegion.lean:124`).

#### [M3] Literature attribution and citation precision: Stoneham (1973), Vandehey Corollary 1.2, and `[2]`
- **Location:** Section 1 ("Earlier results", pp. 2–3, `paper.tex:72–77`), Section 2 (`paper.tex:103–104`), `references.bib`.
- **Evidence:**
  1. **Missing Stoneham (1973) citation:** Page 2 (`paper.tex:73`) refers to *"the usual Stoneham constant with parameter $p$"* and cites only Bailey and Crandall (2002) `[1, Cor. 4.9]`. Stoneham (1973, *Acta Arith.* 22, 277–286 and 371–389) introduced these constants and proved their base-$b$ normality for coprime $(b, p)$ (odd $p$); Bailey–Crandall (2002) gave the unified PRNG proof covering all coprime $b, c > 1$. Omitting Stoneham (1973) from the bibliography is an attribution gap.
  2. **Over-specific formula attributed to Vandehey Corollary 1.2:** Page 3 (`paper.tex:104`) writes: *"Indeed Corollary 1.2 of [4] yields an upper bound $O(B q^{-\delta}(1 + \log q))$ in this range for some $\delta > 0$."* In Vandehey (2019, arXiv:1606.07911), Corollary 1.2 gives explicit iterated-logarithm non-triviality thresholds (around $B \ge \exp(\log q / \log_2 \log q)$); the polynomial-range power saving $O(B q^{-\delta})$ follows by fixing the differencing depth $k$ in Vandehey's Theorem 1.1/5.1, and the extra $(1 + \log q)$ factor is redundant once $\delta > 0$ is existential. Since Lemma 2.1 only needs uniform $o(B)$ cancellation for $q \le B^C$, quoting an unverified exact formula "$O(B q^{-\delta}(1 + \log q))$" from Corollary 1.2 invites a referee objection.
  3. **Qualitative vs. quantitative scope relative to `[2]`:** Page 2 (`paper.tex:75`) rightly notes that `[2]` proves $O(N^{-1/2}\log^3(2N))$ discrepancy for binary $\{2, 3\}$ at order 1. However, the present paper gives *no quantitative discrepancy rate* in $N$ for Theorems 1.1 or 1.2; stating this contrast explicitly alongside "No uniformity in $S$ is asserted" (p. 3, `paper.tex:77`) avoids any appearance of claiming strict dominance over `[2]`'s quantitative bound.
  4. **Unintroduced notation from external GitHub PDF `[2]`:** Page 2 (`paper.tex:73`) writes *"In the sign convention of [2, Eq. (1.1)], the positive series is $-L_S(-z)$"* without defining $L_S$. (Also note: `[2]` is an unrefereed, authorless GitHub PDF; the reviewer could not directly download the PDF at commit `06961817`, so its internal theorem numbers Thms. 1.1, 4.1, 5.1 and Eq. (1.1) should be human-verified before submission.)
  5. **Terse divisibility-chain remark (`paper.tex:75`):** *"On a support containing distinct primes $p, q$, the ordered list of smooth indices contains both $p$ and $q$; their powers $p^s, q^s$ cannot belong to one divisibility chain."* Clarify that Vandehey's Theorem 7.4 requires the *individual term denominators* $c_i$ to satisfy $c_i \mid c_{i+1}$ (preventing cancellation), which fails as soon as $|S| \ge 2$ since neither of $p^s, q^s$ divides the other.
- **No priority overreach detected:** The manuscript makes no improper priority claims over Dibag (1989), Bailey–Crandall (2002), Vandehey (2019), or `[2]`, explicitly credits Vandehey Theorem 7.4 for the unsigned single-axis case, and explicitly disclaims any implication for $\log 2$.

### Minor Mathematical & Structural Findings

#### [m1] Misplaced summation range in definition of $R_n$
- **Location:** Section 2, p. 3 (`paper.tex:80`).
- **Evidence:** *"set $R_n = \sum_{m \le n} a_m b^{n-m}$ for $m \ge 1$."* Writing "for $m \ge 1$" outside the sum reads like a quantifier on a free variable rather than the lower limit of summation (and leaves the range of $n$ unstated, with $a_0$ undefined).
- **Fix:** Write *"set $R_n = \sum_{1 \le m \le n} a_m b^{n-m}$ for $n \ge 0$"*.

#### [m2] "Lemma 2.1 then applies verbatim" is slightly loose for quotient weights
- **Location:** Section 5, p. 7 (`paper.tex:201`).
- **Evidence:** Lemma 2.1 is stated specifically for $X_A(P,s)$ with $a_m = P(m)/m^s$, not for general weights $P(m)/(m^t Q(m))$. The *proof* of Lemma 2.1 applies verbatim (replacing $m^s \mid b^{sH} c^{sH}$ by $m^t |Q(m)| \mid (bc)^{O(\log N)}$), not the lemma statement itself.
- **Fix:** Change to *"The proof of Lemma 2.1 then applies verbatim."* (Or state Lemma 2.1 for any $S$-supported sequence $a_m$ of polynomial growth whose denominators are supported on a fixed finite prime set with exponents $O(\log m)$.)

#### [m3] Missing hypotheses / standing-notation drift in Theorem 1.2, Theorem 5.1, and Corollary 5.2
- **Location:** Section 1 (`paper.tex:57–63`), Section 5 (`paper.tex:175–195`).
- **Evidence:** Theorem 1.1 explicitly quantifies $S, b, s$, whereas Theorem 1.2 omits $S, b$, Theorem 5.1 omits $S, b, s$ and the definition of $r$, and Corollary 5.2 omits the hypotheses of Theorem 5.1 and writes bare $\dd$ instead of $\dd(X_A(P,s))$. #### [m4] Heavy symbol overloading across sections
- **Location:** Throughout `paper.tex` (pp. 1–10).
- **Evidence:** Several core letters change meaning repeatedly within 10 pages:
  - $r$: order of vanishing of $P$ at $0$ (Thm. 1.2, Sec. 5); retained cofactor prime (Sec. 4, p. 5); cofactor integer in $T$ (Lemma A.2 proof, p. 9, while Sec. 3 uses $m$).
  - $d$: denominator order in $U_{p,d}$ (Sec. 3, App. A); number of eliminated cofactor primes (Sec. 4, p. 5).
  - $H$: $\lceil \log_2(N+2B) \rceil$ (Lemma 2.1 proof, p. 3); row separation $(H+1)s > V$ (Thm. 4.1 proof, p. 5); polynomial $H \in \Z[X]$ in $G(m) = G(0) + mH(m)$ (Sec. 5, p. 6); closure of the log flow in $(\R/\Z)^J$ (Cor. A.3, p. 9).
  - $T$: $M(S \setminus \{p\})$ (Sec. 3, p. 4); forward-difference stride $(p-1)p^a$ (Lemma A.2 proof, p. 9); phase representative $1 \le T_p \le p$ (Cor. A.3, p. 9).
  - $t$: normalized phase in $[1,p)$ (Secs. 3, 4, 6, App. A); residual pole order $t = s - r$ (Sec. 5, pp. 6–7).
  - $u$: block start (Lemma 2.1, p. 3); cutoff phase (Eq. (5), p. 4); principal unit $1 + pz$ (Lemma A.2 proof, p. 9); integration variable (Cor. A.3, p. 9).
  - $E$: failed set $E_{p,d,L}$ (Sec. 3, p. 4); periodic exponent residue set (Cor. 5.2, p. 7); arbitrary measurable set (Thm. A.1 proof, p. 9).
  - $C$: polynomial exponent $q_u \le B^C$ (Lemma 2.1, p. 3); certificate $C_{p,d,L}$ (Secs. 3–6); valuation bound $C(b,p,s)$ (Lemma A.2, p. 8).
- **Recommendation:** Reserve $t$ for the real phase (use $s' = s - r$ or $\sigma$ in Sec. 5), reserve $d$ for order (use $\ell$ or $k$ for the number of eliminated primes in Sec. 4), use $m$ consistently for elements of $T$ in Lemma A.2's proof, and avoid reusing $H$ four times (e.g. $\mathcal{H}$ or $\overline{\mathcal{O}}$ for the flow closure in Cor. A.3).
