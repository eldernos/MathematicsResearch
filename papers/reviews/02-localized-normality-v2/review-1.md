A retained block has no insertion in its interior and has buffers for all previous and next insertions. The age ≥ B clears all radix factors of the common denominator, whose exponents are O(log N), including those from coefficient denominators. Every coefficient grows at most polynomially and the future buffer gives a uniform exponential tail bound. The off-radix denominator at the block start is ≤ B^C after increasing C for fixed data. If h is a fixed nonzero frequency, the reduced denominator q of hR_u lies between q_u/|h| and q_u. Its prime support remains fixed, (q,b) = 1, and its reduced numerator a is coprime to q. Vandehey's sum starts at exponent 1. Replace its numerator by a b⁻¹ mod q for the block starting at exponent 0.
