## Owner-directed addition: Phase 19 refinement

This item is not a referee finding. §5.1, Proposition 5.2 (*Paper proof*), states that the sums dD\* of d elements of {0} ∪ {2^(−i)} form a compact countable set, so the open plateaus have full measure, M_d is constant on each plateau and its distribution is purely atomic. It also states that nonvanishing on every plateau suffices for normality of all rational polynomials of degree d in α(b,2), with a complete transfer proof through failed sets E_L of measure tending to zero.

Remark 5.3 records three further facts. The test holds in the degrees of Theorems 4.1 and 4.3, fails in degree 2 and is undetermined elsewhere. The curvature argument in notes/phase19/nonlinear.md proves that every level set of the synthetic square mass is null, and that conclusion is false for the dyadic mass in degree 2 ({M₂ = 0} ⊇ [3/2, 2)), so the argument does not apply to α(b,2)² − 2α(b,2). For that quadratic the relevant denominator depends on 2-adic digits of b^(2^i) at growing depth. Normality of α(b,2)² − 2α(b,2) for every odd radix and whole-shell nonvanishing of M_d for general d ≥ 3 are stated as open in the abstract, at the end of §3, after Theorem 5.1 and in Remark 5.3.

## Questions reserved for a human specialist

1. **Lemma 6.2.** Is the support-dependent repair of Vandehey's induction complete? In particular, does the gcd identity gcd(q, b^(iτ) − 1) = g·gcd(i, q/g) hold at every unsaturated prime, including 2? Are the constants uniform when an inner modulus in (11) loses a prime? Does the journal version of Theorem 5.1 already correct equation (12)?
2. **Theorem 6.1.** Check the piece-length moment on the last incomplete shell and the harmonic gcd average after frequency reduction. Check that δ_d is the exponent obtained at depth d − 1 (no optimality is claimed).
3. **Theorems 4.3 and Lemmas 4.5–4.6.** Check rule B (at most d binary ones), the limit cuts approached from below, and evaluation of the cyclic-descent congruence only after the Hilbert-series denominator is cleared. Check that the reduced automata used by the second verifier compute the masses of real cuts; only the (3, 31) automaton is checked in Lean.
4. **Theorem 5.1.** Check completeness of the classification of minimizers, especially at d = 2^r − 1. Are there antecedents in the literature on Kraft-inequality or optimal prefix codes?
5. **Proposition 5.2.** Check that the endpoints of the failed sets are insertion points, so that each insertion-free block lies on one side, and check the density bound 2|E_L|. Can the plateau values of M_d be determined in any degree d ≥ 3 not covered by the certificates?
6. **Resonant quadratic.** Do the upper-half denominators 2^max(0, 2k−1−v₂(Z_k)) of α(b,2)² − 2α(b,2) tend to infinity for even one fixed odd radix b?
7. **Priority.** Can a MathSciNet search, or a specialist, find earlier statements of Theorems 3.1, 3.2 or 5.1? Does the inclusion–exclusion argument of Proposition 2.1 capture the same integrality obstruction as S08's high/low decomposition for every periodic phase?
