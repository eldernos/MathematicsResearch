For coprime integers b,c ≥ 2, let α be the Stoneham series with terms 1/(c^j b^(c^j)) for j ≥ 1. Its dth power, expanded in ordered tuples, has a new insertion whenever a sum of d powers of c reaches the current digit position. Between changes its digits follow a rational radix orbit. For c > 2, the key arithmetic fact is that a periodic geometric sum has complex conjugates of modulus strictly between zero and one. A monic equation would make it integral, so the leading term in each reflected prefix retains a growing denominator. This proves normality for every rational polynomial of positive degree, as in Mira Cult’s S08, Theorem 1.1, and gives simultaneous digit frequencies by the character criterion. At c = 2 one square cut has zero mass. The square has an exact two-piece denominator profile. Finite partitions and cyclic descent cover degrees 3 through 120, degree 128 and sixteen infinite families. The resonant quadratic α² − 2α remains open in any fixed odd radix.
