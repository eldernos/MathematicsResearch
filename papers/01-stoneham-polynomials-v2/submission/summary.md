For coprime integers b,c ≥ 2, let α be the Stoneham series with terms 1/(c^j b^(c^j)) for j ≥ 1. Mira Cult’s public, unrefereed notes S08 (Theorem 1.1 and Corollaries 1.2–1.3) prove that for c > 2 every nonconstant rational polynomial in α is normal in base b and that distinct powers of α are jointly normal; S08 Theorem 1.1(b) also gives an effective power saving with an unspecified exponent. This manuscript gives a complete kernel-verified Lean 4 proof of that ordinary case and the explicit scalar exponent 1/(2(2^(d+1) − 2)) in degree d. The dth power of α, expanded in ordered tuples, gains a new term whenever a sum of d powers of c reaches the current digit position; between such insertions its digits follow a rational radix orbit. For c > 2, a periodic geometric sum has complex conjugates of modulus strictly between zero and one, so it is not an algebraic integer; inclusion–exclusion expresses the leading prefix mass on every reflected cut as a monic polynomial in that sum, so the leading denominators lose only a bounded power of each prime of c, and the character criterion gives the digit frequencies. At c = 2 one square cut has zero mass, yet the square has an exact two-piece denominator profile and is normal. Finite partitions and cyclic descent cover degrees 3 through 120, degree 128 and sixteen infinite families; the least 2-adic valuation of the leading mass is determined in every degree; and nonvanishing of that mass on the open plateaus of the shell, which have full measure, would suffice in any given degree. The resonant quadratic α² − 2α remains open in every odd radix, and the curvature argument that settles certain synthetic schedules does not apply to it.
