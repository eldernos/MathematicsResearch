## External analytic dependencies

- **Theorem 6.3 (transcendence part only):** Ridout's restricted-denominator theorem (D. Ridout, *Rational approximations to algebraic numbers*, Mathematika 4 (1957), 125–131; and *The $p$-adic generalization of the Thue–Siegel–Roth theorem*, Mathematika 5 (1958), 40–48), used in the standard form: for a real algebraic irrational $\alpha$, a finite set $S$ of primes, and $\epsilon>0$, only finitely many rationals $u/t$ ($t\ge1$) with $t$ supported on $S$ satisfy $|\alpha-u/t|<t^{-1-\epsilon}$. The exact page-level theorem locator in the 1957/1958 papers has not been verified from primary text.
- **All other theorems, propositions, lemmas, and corollaries** are self-contained given the kernel-verified `SmoothOrbit.fixed_support_orbit_estimate` (which gives an internal qualitative differencing proof of the fixed-support short-orbit estimate rather than invoking Vandehey 2019 as a black box) and elementary $p$-adic / Fourier analysis (Mahler 1958 and Robert 2000 are cited for standard context on $p$-adic binomial interpolation, while the required bound $v_p(\nabla^k g(0))\ge k+1$ and uniform convergence are proved directly in Proposition 3.1).
