| Corollary 10 | Paper deduction. Compactness and countability give a location gap on every single-prime shell, normality rules out a rational limit, and Ridout applies. The conclusion concerns values of Theorem 2, including products of different dilations and strides. |
| Proposition 11 | Paper deduction for two multiplicatively independent parameters each coprime to the base. Irrational rotation supplies bounded gaps between separated insertions. The conclusion is strict discrepancy below one and transcendence; it gives neither the explicit 43/45 for general inputs nor their full normality. |
