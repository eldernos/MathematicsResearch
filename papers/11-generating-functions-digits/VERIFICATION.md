## Evidence labels

- **Kernel-verified**: the complete statement is a Lean 4 declaration checked by the kernel.
- **Lean-supported paper proof**: a written proof whose finite core is a kernel-verified declaration; the remaining steps are written.
- **Paper proof**: a written proof only.
- **External input**: a published theorem quoted without proof.
- **Finite certificate**: an exact finite computation. The manuscript quotes finite computations only as illustrations; no numbered statement rests on one, so this label is not assigned to any theorem.

## Series versus products (read this first)

Lean defines the **series**, coefficient by coefficient, and never the products.

- [`eulerPentagonalSum`](https://github.com/eldernos/MathematicsResearch/blob/347ec3fe631a3e7ab2ae25d53508a3a4c9ea9fa8/NormalityChecker/EulerPentagonal.lean#L133) `b` is `∑ pentCoeff(m) b^(−m)`, where `pentCoeff 0 = 1`, `pentCoeff m = (−1)^k` for a chosen witness `k ≥ 1` with `m = k(3k−1)/2` or `m = k(3k+1)/2`, and `0` otherwise. That the witness is unique (so that the series is exactly `1 + Σ (−1)^k (b^(−P_k^−) + b^(−P_k^+))`) is immediate from strict monotonicity but is not stated as a Lean theorem.
- [`eulerCubeSum`](https://github.com/eldernos/MathematicsResearch/blob/347ec3fe631a3e7ab2ae25d53508a3a4c9ea9fa8/NormalityChecker/EulerPentagonal.lean#L355) `b` is Jacobi's triangular series `Σ_{k ≥ 0} (−1)^k (2k+1) b^(−k(k+1)/2)`.
- **Euler's pentagonal number theorem** `∏(1 − q^k) = 1 + Σ (−1)^k (q^{P_k^−} + q^{P_k^+})` and **Jacobi's identity** `∏(1 − q^k)^3 = Σ (−1)^k (2k+1) q^{T_k}` are classical external inputs (Theorem 4.1). Neither is formalized.
- No Lean theorem uses the signs of these coefficients: each holds for every coefficient sequence with the same support and the same absolute values.
- Every statement in the paper about the products `E_b` and `E_b^3` is the Lean statement about the series transported through Theorem 4.1. **Nothing about `E_b^2` is formalized** (no definition, support count or kernel statement).
- The finite Lean bound `eulerPentagonal_finite_gap_upper` has the right order but leading constant `2√2`, a factor `2√3` above the sharp constant `√(2/3)` of Theorem 4.6. The sharp upper bound uses the kernel-verified general lemma `Phase18Synthetic.sparse_farCount_le`; its instantiation to the pentagonal positions is a written step.

## What the Lean statements do not cover

Lean does not check: Euler's or Jacobi's identity; any statement about `E_b^2`; the sharp non-lookahead shadow count and the explicit rates of §3 (Lean has `L·S(N+L)` and the qualitative limits); the digit formulas and word-frequency bounds of §4; any analytic number theory input (Landau, Bernays, Wirsing, Shiu, Selberg–Delange, Brun, Chebotarev, Deligne–Serre); the exact constants of §5; the dichotomy and oriented-tail theorems of §7; the sieve of §8; the wall of §9.

The working tree also contains three uncommitted modules: `NormalityChecker/SimpleNormalityKernel.lean` (its docstring states finite versions of Lemmas 7.1–7.2, Theorems 7.3–7.4 and Lemma 7.5(a)–(b)), `NormalityChecker/RadixPowerTower.lean` and `NormalityChecker/Phase22Cores.lean`. The registry entries GF-19, GF-20 and GF-23 list declarations from them. None of the three is pinned at a commit, and none was built or audited for this draft, so `paper.tex` cites none of them and labels the corresponding statements **Paper proof**. Once they are committed and pass `lake build` and the axiom audit, the labels of Theorems 7.3–7.4 and Lemma 7.5 may be raised to Lean-supported paper proof, with links pinned to the new commit and line numbers checked against it.
