A preprint on binary-shift approximations proves balanced trace denominator rates of 5/12 and 13/30 for ln 2, and gives Legendre rounding constructions for ln 2 and π. The results concern rational approximants and leave limiting digit distribution open.

## Mathematics forum

This revised preprint studies the reduced denominators of rational models for binary shifts. The main theorem reduces balanced algebraic traces at every cutoff to real and Gaussian logarithm sources and identifies the two smallest rates, 5/12 and 13/30, for ln 2. A Lean certificate covers the finite prime-band comparison. Separate written arguments give Legendre moment rounding for ln 2 and π and explain the limits of the resulting shadowing windows. I would welcome feedback on the all-cutoff reduction and the relation of the prescribed-shift moment construction to classical Padé approximation.
