## Mathematics forum

This revised preprint studies the reduced denominators of rational models for binary shifts of ln 2. The main theorem reduces balanced algebraic traces at every cutoff to real and Gaussian logarithm sources and identifies the two smallest rates, 5/12 and 13/30, among fixed finite identities truncated at a common binary scale. A Lean certificate covers the finite prime-band comparison. The floors concern truncation without rounding. A second part rounds Legendre polynomial moments on a least-common-multiple grid for ln 2 and pi. Those models are nearest-grid roundings, which exist for every real number, so their denominator exponents are a choice of grid and are not compared with the floors. The paper proves neither normality nor a limiting discrepancy bound below one for ln 2, pi, e, sqrt 2 or the golden ratio. I would welcome feedback on the primitive-divisor descent, whose method is that of Borwein, Borwein and Galway, and on the relation of the prescribed-shift moment construction to the restricted-denominator Padé results of Rivoal.
