### A, 2.1–2.2 and 3.1: Balanced trace reduction and least denominator rates

- **Statement and hypotheses:** Let $S$ be a fixed finite rational linear combination of trace prefixes with monic integer source polynomials, each with roots $2^{a/e}\zeta$ for one nonnegative $a/e$ and roots of unity $\zeta$. Truncate each at $j(t-a/e)\le x$, with $t>a/e$ integral. Theorem 3.1 reduces each to integer combinations of $A_k,G_j$ at every real cutoff. For a fixed such combination with limit $\log 2$, $Y_N=2^NS(N)$ has error $O((N+1)^{-1})$. For any fixed finite rational $A,G$ combination the reduced rate is $\sum_{v\ge1:S(v/2)\ne0}2/[v(v+1)]$. The minimum in the real class is $1/2$, attained only by $2A_2+A_3-A_6$. In the balanced class the two smallest rates are $5/12$ and $13/30$, attained uniquely by $2A_2+6A_3+10A_4-6A_6-5G_3$ and $2A_2+3A_3+4A_4-3A_6-2G_3$, respectively.

### 5.1–5.2: Bézout moment grids and the Chebyshev floor

- **Statement and hypotheses:** Integer polynomial moments of degree $<D$ (with the substitution $x^2$ in the odd-grid case) fill the full lattice $L^{-1}\mathbb Z$ with $L=\operatorname{lcm}(1,\ldots,D)$ or $L=\operatorname{lcm}(1,3,\ldots,2D-1)$ and realize nearest-grid rounding of rational moments. For a fixed integral identity $\log 2=\sum_i c_i\int_0^1(a_i-\sigma_i x)^{-1}dx$ with positive denominators and nonzero grouped coefficients, let $\rho=\min_i(z_i+\sqrt{z_i^2-1})$ with $z_i=|2\sigma_i a_i-1|$. The $T_D(2x-1)$ construction has shifted error $O(1/N)$ for all sufficiently large $N$ and upper log-denominator rate $\log 2/\log\rho$. If the nearest pole is unique, the analytic residual at $N=\lfloor\log_2\min_i|T_D(2\sigma_i a_i-1)|\rfloor$ has magnitude $\asymp D^{-2}$, stable under $O(D^{-3})$ moment perturbations.

### 5.3–5.5: All-shift Legendre construction, nearest-grid benchmark and identification

- **Statement and hypotheses:** For the fixed $\log 2$ pole identity in 5.2, every sufficiently large $N$ admits a rational model with $q_N\mid L_D$, shifted error at most $(2L_D)^{-1}+O(\rho^{-D})$, and $D/N\to1/\log_2\rho$. ### 7.3 and 8.1: Positive factorial and Taylor grouping

- **Statement and hypotheses:** A finite subset of the Taylor terms $1/m!$ of $e$ with maximum index $M\ge3$ has a nondyadic sum. For any partition of $1/(m2^m)$ into finite groups of dyadic sum, let $M(p)$ be the maximum index in the group containing the term at the prime $p$. Then $\liminf_{p\ {\rm prime},\,p\to\infty}M(p)/(p\log p)\ge1/(1+\log 2)$. ### 8.2: Geometric ray partitions and finite joint uniformity

- **Statement and hypotheses:** The positive integers partition into rays $rp^j$, $j\ge0$, with distinct odd primes across rays, yielding $\log 2=\sum_{\rm rays}\sum_{j\ge0}2^{-rp^j}/(rp^j)$. The union of the four positive BBP denominator classes $a=1,4,5,6$ modulo 8 partitions into such rays with $p\equiv1\bmod8$, giving $\pi=\sum_{\rm rays}(c_a/r)\sum_{j\ge0}16^{-(rp^j-a)/8}/p^j$ with $c_a=4,-2,-1,-1$ respectively. Both identities are absolutely convergent. Any fixed finite selection from either partition has a jointly uniform binary orbit.

### 9.1–9.2: Exact positive completion and mean denominator loss

- **Statement and hypotheses:** For odd prime $p\ge5$, let $k(p)$ be the least largest multiplier in a subset of $[1,p-1]$ containing 1 with $\sum_{j\in S}(j2^j)^{-1}=0\bmod p$. The least last index of a finite positive dyadic Taylor group containing $1/(p2^p)$ is $p\,k(p)$, and an attaining group has dyadic exponent $p\,k(p)+v_2(k(p))$. For $p=3$ the least index is 12. For $p\ge41$, $k(p)\le\lceil3\sqrt{4p-3}\rceil$. In $(X/2,X]$ the lower bound $k(p)\ge\log_2p-2\log_2\log_2p$ holds except on a relative $O(1/\log X)$ of primes. For full reduced denominators $Q_n$ of the ordinary shifted Taylor prefixes, the mean of $\log(L_n/Q_n)$ over $n\le X$ is $O(X^{3/7})$.

### 9.3–9.4: Taylor periods and the signed moving cutoff
