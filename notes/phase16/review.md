# Independent review and verification

The starting tag `research-checkpoint-15` is commit `17359df`. Every Phase 16 track underwent an independent cross-provider mathematical and computational audit in addition to Lean 4.36.0 compilation, axiom auditing and kernel replay on Hail.

## Independent review of Tracks 1, 3 and 5 (Codex)

An independent Codex audit ran all three verification scripts—including the extended 2²⁴ Erdős–Borwein prefix check (`0.494216`), the full degree-120 Stoneham certificate (118 rows and 590 exact witness values), and 20 cyclic-descent boundary checks—and inspected every proof line by line.

- **Track 1 (Erdős–Borwein correlations):** Confirmed the *p*-adic joint law, local Haar averages, covariance bounds and small-frequency limit. Flagged four items that were revised in the final note: (1) adding the explicit finite gap cutoff *K*<sub>*I*</sub> = 2 log₂ *N* + *b* + 2 justifying termwise integration in Theorem 1(2); (2) supplying the uniform *O*(1/*P*) Euler-product tail estimate passing from finite CRT averages to the full product; (3) correcting the Gaussian exponent in Corollary 3.1 from −8π²θ²*T* to −4π²θ²*T*; (4) clarifying the two-form sieve route for external input K2, quantifying the summed smoothing error in Theorem 3, separating the marked and *r*-point extensions as remarks, splitting far gaps in Theorem 4 via the Shiu/Rankin exponential moment, correcting the Tao–Teräväinen citation to Theorem 1.6 (with §4.1 and *L*<sub>*N*</sub> ≍ *T*<sup>1/4</sup>) and Helfgott–Radziwiłł's almost-all-scales natural average, qualifying the word-uniformity of Mira's scaling estimate, and noting that Crandall's base-4/base-16 tables are internally consistent with his binary counts so any bit-complement swap would have to occur upstream. [Revised note](erdos-borwein-correlations.md)
- **Track 3 (Adaptive logarithm identities):** Confirmed the 5/12 − 1/*V* cell lemma, all four linear-height thresholds, and the 60-periodic limit min |*g*<sub>*c*</sub>| = 1/15. Identified two substantive scope items that were tightened: (1) restricting both the 11/60 one-parameter floor and the Proposition 4 error obstruction to **integer** *c*<sub>*N*</sub> ∈ ℤ, and explicitly recording the rational counterexample *c*<sub>*N*</sub> = (round(2<sup>*N*</sup> ln 2) − 2<sup>*N*</sup> *B*(*N*))/*Y*<sub>*c*</sub>(*N*) giving *q*<sub>*N*</sub> = 1 and error ≤ 1/2; (2) correcting the touching signed-alphabet source count from 16 to 14, restricting the prime-support equivalence in §3 to *p* ∤ *D*<sub>*N*</sub>, choosing CRT representatives in [*M*, 2*M*) for the height asymptotic, and framing the 2D small-error examples as finite exact instances. The 730-node branch-and-bound certificate was also formalized and kernel-verified in `NormalityChecker/AdaptiveLogarithmBounds.lean`. [Revised note](adaptive-logarithms.md) · [Lean certificate](../../NormalityChecker/AdaptiveLogarithmBounds.lean)
- **Track 5 (Dyadic Stoneham polynomials):** Confirmed the functional equations, function-field transcendence, 2-adic isometry (with positive derivative), digit automaton, cyclic descent, and Kraft-point formula. Flagged that the headline equality *v*₂(*Z*<sub>*k*</sub>) = *k* + *s* + *v*₂(*k* − κ₀) fails above the threshold *v*₂(*k* − κ₀) ≥ *k* − 1 + *s* (e.g. *b* = 5, *k* = 3 where *Z*₃ = −3061248 has *v*₂ = 9 vs. 10, while both give *q*₃ = 1). The final note and script carry the exact threshold throughout, tighten σ to an injective endomorphism of *F*<sub>*m*</sub>, clarify the cyclic-descent rescaling boundary, add the strict monotonicity of balanced Kraft sums in Proposition B.8, add *v*₂(β) ≥ *s* and the union over *c* in Corollary A.5, and fix the auxiliary-function gain typo to 2<sup>−(*n*+*s*)*T*</sup>. [Revised note](dyadic-stoneham.md) · [Script](../../scripts/phase16_dyadic_stoneham.py)

The final note writes out the complete deterministic `#planted − #bad` proof of Theorem 3.3 (including injectivity without second moments, Selberg sieve over inert primes, and Brun–Titchmarsh tail bound), restricts *f*<sub>sym</sub> cleanly to a symmetric Poisson model benchmark, and fixes all five minor items.
