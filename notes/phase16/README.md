# Phase 16 — adaptive logarithm rates, weighted Lambert benchmarks, dyadic Stoneham degrees and mixed-product sparsity

**Three headline advances across classical logarithms and Stoneham arithmetic.**

1. **Adaptive 5/12 obstruction and its explicit escape.** For any sequence of formulas *S*<sup>(*N*)</sup> for ln 2 in the full balanced algebraic trace alphabet (of arbitrary degree and arbitrary rational binary exponent *a*/*e*), collapsing at each cutoff *N* to θ<sub>*N*</sub> ∈ ℚ⁴ of height *H*<sub>*N*</sub>, subexponential height log *H*<sub>*N*</sub> = *o*(*N*) preserves the fixed-formula denominator rate identically: (log *q*<sub>*N*</sub>)/*N* − ρ(θ<sub>*N*</sub>) → 0, forcing liminf (log *q*<sub>*N*</sub>)/*N* ≥ 5/12 with no limit point in (5/12, 13/30). Lean kernel-verifies the 730-node branch-and-bound certificate over all of ℚ⁴. Outside the balanced trace alphabet, explicit ln 2-free rounded Chebyshev-pole integrals break 5/12 with shifted error *O*(1/*N*), reaching rate ≤ 0.393220 (single pole), ≤ 0.263162 (two-stride), and arbitrarily close to zero (*M*-pole telescoping), though without homogeneous shift persistence. [Adaptive obstruction](adaptive-logarithms.md) · [Balanced completeness & Chebyshev poles](nonflat-approximants.md) · [Lean global cell bound](../../NormalityChecker/AdaptiveLogarithmBounds.lean) · [Lean Chebyshev bounds](../../NormalityChecker/ChebyshevPoleApproximation.lean)
2. **Dyadic Stoneham degrees 3–120 and seven infinite families.** Every rational polynomial *P*(α(*b*, 2)) of degree 3 through 120 (up from 15 in Phase 14), and every degree in the seven infinite cyclic-descent families 3·31ʳ, 3·127ʳ, 3·257ʳ, 3·337ʳ, 3·1801ʳ, 3·8191ʳ and 4·8191ʳ (*r* ≥ 0), is normal in every odd base *b* ≥ 3. For the remaining quadratic obstruction α² − 2α, Lean verifies the elementary integer recurrence *Z*₁ = 2 − 2*b*², *Z*<sub>*k*+1</sub> = 2 + 2*b*<sup>2ᵏ</sup>(*Z*<sub>*k*</sub> − 1) governing *q*<sub>*k*</sub> = 2<sup>max(0, 2*k*−1−*v*₂(*Z*<sub>*k*</sub>))</sup>, while paper proofs establish the base-change functional equation, function-field transcendence and bijective 2-adic isometry of κ₀(λ). [Dyadic Stoneham analysis](dyadic-stoneham.md) · [Lean quadratic & mixed-product bounds](../../NormalityChecker/Phase16StonehamBounds.lean)
3. **Mixed-prime Stoneham product within 2.85 × 10⁻¹⁰ of 4/11.** Certified multi-depth non-coincidence across 315 bulk shapes at depths (*h*, *k*) = (9, 7) cuts the unconditional upper binary discrepancy bound for α(2, 3) α(2, 5) from Phase 14's 0.37048 to 4/11 + 2.85 × 10⁻¹⁰ < 0.363636364. Simultaneous ultrametric separation proves power-tower spacing between successive bounded-denominator shell intervals and super-exponential sparsity of high-discrepancy cutoffs: for every ε > 0, the number of integer scales *m* ≤ *T* containing any *N* ∈ [*e*ᵐ, *e*ᵐ⁺¹) with *D*<sub>*N*</sub> > ε is *O*<sub>ε</sub>(log* *T*), and Σ<sub>*D*<sub>*N*</sub>>ε</sub> 1/(*N* log *N*) < ∞. [Mixed-product precision and sparsity](mixed-product-precision.md)
