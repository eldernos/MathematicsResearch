# Phase 20. Arithmetic generating functions and digit-distribution transfer

In any integer radix $b \ge 2$, let $F_b(a) = \sum_{m \ge 1} a(m) b^{-m}$ be an integer-coefficient series with cumulative mass $A(X) = \sum_{m \le X} |a(m)|$ and support count $S(X) = \#\{1 \le m \le X : a(m) \ne 0\}$. Whenever $A(X) = O(X)$ and $S(X) = o(X)$, the extreme discrepancy satisfies $D_N(F_b(a); b) \to 1$, so $F_b(a)$ belongs to the numerical translation kernel $K_b$ and preserves every input's discrepancy under addition. Lean verifies this linear-mass sparse transfer, composite-radix displaced factorization $a(m) = b^V U$, and a two-sided circle separation lemma. On paper, the all-$N$ linear-mass rate has sharp leading constant $2$, displaced widths reduce the constant to $1$ under bounded mean $|U|$, and isolated sources obey an exact leading constant law. ## Euler products, theta values and weight-one modular forms

- **Euler's product and low powers.** For every $b \ge 2$, Lean verifies that Euler's pentagonal value $E_b = \prod_{k \ge 1}(1 - b^{-k})$ and its cube $E_b^3$ belong to $K_b$ and preserve every input's discrepancy. On paper, $E_b$ has explicit base-$b$ digit blocks, word frequencies $1/2$ for $0^L$ and $(b-1)^L$ with explicit all-prefix error, is never disjunctive, and satisfies $1 - D_N(E_b; b) = \sqrt{2/3}\,(\log_b N)/\sqrt{N} + O_b(1/\sqrt{N})$. The cube $E_b^3$ is base-$b$ **disjunctive** with $1 - D_N(E_b^3; b) = \sqrt{2}\,(\log_b N)/\sqrt{N} + O_b(1/\sqrt{N})$. The square $E_b^2$ is governed by the weight-one Klein-four CM eigenform $\eta(12\tau)^2$ (Serre exponent $\lambda = 3/4$), giving $1 - D_N(E_b^2; b) \asymp (\log\log N)/(\log N)^{3/4}$. Every polynomial $P \in \mathbb Z[1/b][x]$ of degree $\le 3$ maps $E_b$ into $K_b$, whereas $(a/d)E_b \in K_b \iff a/d \in \mathbb Z[1/b]$. [Euler product note](euler-product.md) · [Lean module](../../NormalityChecker/EulerPentagonal.lean)
- **Binary quadratic-form theta values and weight-one modular forms.** Every positive definite integral binary quadratic form $Q$ satisfies $\Theta_Q(1/b) \in K_b$ with $1 - D_N(\Theta_Q(1/b); b) \ll_{Q,b} (\log\log N)/\sqrt{\log N}$ and a matching lower bound, including imprimitive forms, shifted lattices, congruence subsums, bounded weights, and degree-$\le 2$ polynomials in unary quadratic-polynomial series. More generally, **every** holomorphic weight-one modular form $f(q) = \sum_{n \ge 0} a(n) q^n$ on $\Gamma_1(M)$ with integer coefficients (dihedral CM, exotic $A_4/S_4/A_5$, and weight-one Eisenstein series) satisfies $f(1/b) \in K_b$ with $1 - D_N(f(1/b); b) \ll_{f,b} (\log\log N)/(\log N)^{\alpha_f}$ via Deligne–Serre and Chebotarev. For Serre's lacunary even $\eta$-powers $\eta^r$ ($r \in \{4,6,8,10,14,26\}$), coefficient transfer fails structurally due to radix-digit overlap despite zero coefficient density. [Theta and modular note](theta-and-modular.md)
- **Exact Lambert leading constant.** Upgrading Phase 17's order theorem (`CC-48`) in base 2,
  ```math
  1 - D_N(L_{\chi_4}; 2) = \sup_x |D_N(x + L_{\chi_4}; 2) - D_N(x; 2)| \sim \frac{K}{2 \log 2} \, \frac{\log\log N}{\sqrt{\log N}},
  ```
  where $K = 0.76422365\dots$ is the Landau–Ramanujan constant; the same asymptotic holds for $\Theta_3(1/2)^2 = 1 + 4L_{\chi_4}$, and with $C_\chi/(2\log 2)$ for every real nonprincipal Dirichlet character $\chi$ (with explicit radix-scope contrast for odd bases such as $b = 3$). [Leading constant proof](lambert-leading-constant.md)

## Dense series, partitions, classical transfers and polynomial normality

- **Dense $q$-series, partitions and $K_b$ operations.** Carry-window transfer reduces $D_N(F_b(a))$ to finite modular sums $R_{n,L} \bmod b^L$ with certified windows ($O(\log N)$ for polynomial growth; $(\pi\sqrt{2/3}/\log b)\sqrt{N} + O(\log N)$ for the partition constant $P_b = 1/E_b = \sum p(n)b^{-n}$). Ramanujan congruences on $p(m)$ wash out from base-$b$ digits; distributional properties of $P_b$, $\Theta_3(1/b)^3$, $\Theta_3(1/b)^4$, $E_{2,4,6}(1/b)$, $\Delta(1/b)$, and $E_b^{2r}$ ($r \ge 2$) remain open. Exact kernel transfers give $\Theta_3(1/b)^4 \equiv 16 L_{\chi_4}(b)^2$ and $\Theta_3(1/b)^3 \equiv 4 \Theta_3(1/b) L_{\chi_4}(b) \pmod{K_b}$. Inside $K_b$, squaring fails closure ($x = U + V \in K_b, x^2 \notin K_b$), radix-primary rationals $A/b^j$ satisfy $b^j/A \in K_b \iff v \mid b-1$, and arbitrarily lacunary $z$ exist with $1 - z \in K_2$ yet $\liminf D_N(1/(1-z); 2) = 0$. [Dense series and partitions](dense-and-partitions.md)
- **Classical transfers and honest status.** Exact mod-8 character identities (kernel-verified in [`CharacterLambertTransfer.lean`](../../NormalityChecker/CharacterLambertTransfer.lean)) and general 2-elementary quotient transfers equate the binary discrepancy asymptotics of $A_1, A_3, A_5, A_7$ and $E_{\rm odd}$ up to $O((\log\log N)/\sqrt{\log N})$. The logarithmic Euler-product formula $\ln 2 = \sum \mu(m) F(2^{-m})$ collapses chronologically to the ordinary Taylor prefixes and inherits `OP-11`. Sharp translation produces explicit normal numbers $\alpha(b, c) + Z$ with $Z \in K_b$ from modular/theta sources. Nothing distributional is proved for $\ln 2, \pi, e, \sqrt{2}, \varphi, E$, or $P_b$. [Classical transfers](classical-transfers.md)
- **All-prime polynomial normality and higher-degree schedules.** The protected-pole series $W = \sum_{k \ge 1} 1/(p^{k^2}\ell_k b^{2^{k^3}})$ has every nonconstant $P(W)$ base-$b$ normal and Liouville with super-logarithmic rate $\exp(-c(\log N)^{2/3})$ (denominator protection kernel-verified in [`ProtectedPole.lean`](../../NormalityChecker/ProtectedPole.lean)), generalizing to single-generator, shared-prime, and distinct-odd-prime + auxiliary-prime protected-pole algebras (`GF-10`). On power schedules $t k^d$, constant-measure dominant-anchor isolation and Titchmarsh's $q$-th derivative test ($q = 4, \dots, r+1$, exact uniform margin $\sigma_r = 1/(r(2^{r+1}-2))$) prove that every nonconstant rational polynomial of degree $r < d$ in $C(b,p,s,d)$ is base-$b$ normal (`GF-11`; boundary $d = r$ remains open). [All-prime polynomial normality](all-prime-polynomials.md) · [Higher-degree power schedules](cubic-power-schedule.md)
