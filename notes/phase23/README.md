## Phase 22 consolidation corrections and deep analytic audits (Stage 0–1)

Four consolidation corrections from Phase 22 were locked down by regression tests ([`scripts/test_phase22_corrections.py`](../../scripts/test_phase22_corrections.py)):

2. **Exact `E_+` coefficient formula (`GF-28`, `BC-39`):** `a_+(n) = (1 − v_2(n)) τ_odd(n)`.
3. **Strict construction-radix scope (`GF-19`, `GF-20`, `BF-11`, `BF-16`, Paper XI):** Qualified all sparse-transfer digit claims to their matching construction radix `b`.
4. **Explicit `GF-25` Theorem G hypotheses (`GF-25`, `BF-20`):** Stated `p > (r + 1)!`, `λ ≥ r + 1`, and `(P_G)` explicitly.
