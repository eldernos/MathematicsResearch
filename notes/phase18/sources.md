# Phase 18 Track G3: Primary Literature, Historical Priority and Source Verification

This note records directly verified primary bibliographic records, exact mathematical scope, and an honest priority assessment for the topics of Phase 18 (Tracks A–F): Stoneham/Korobov/Kano/Bailey–Crandall constructions, Vandehey's sparse arithmetic series and short-orbit estimates, normal Liouville numbers, Rauzy's deterministic numbers, Bergelson and Downarowicz's ergodic theory of arithmetic operations (including their June 2025 preprint), symbolic constructions with prescribed invariant measures and discrepancy, and recent 2026 developments by John M. Campbell, Captain Sude and Mira Cult.

**Verification protocol.** Every DOI, arXiv record, and GitHub repository listed below was checked directly on **25 November 2026** via the Crossref API, arXiv abstract/HTML records, and the GitHub API. Where a legacy translation DOI could not be confirmed by Crossref (e.g., Korobov 1990 *Math. Notes* translation) or a paper remains a preprint (Bergelson–Downarowicz `arXiv:2506.12929`), that status is marked explicitly rather than inferred. **A literature check that finds no exact match never proves historical novelty**; priority classifications below remain provisional pending independent expert review.

| **G. Rauzy (1976)** | *Nombres normaux et processus déterministes*, Acta Arithmetica **29**(3) (1976), 211–225. **Priority note on Track B (B1–B4):** Realizing every $\delta \in [0, 1]$ or every $T_b$-invariant probability measure by **digit-block concatenation** is classical (Colebrook 1970, Sigmund 1974, Denker–Grillenberger–Sigmund 1976). Track B's distinctive contribution is realizing every $\delta \in [0, 1]$ (with explicit quantitative convergence $O(N^{-1/3})$) and mixtures of rational orbits via **positive rational-prefix denominator programming** $\sum c_k b^{-n_k}$ (or dense geometric-retirement series) rather than direct digit concatenation—and contrasting this full interval $[0, 1]$ with Paper III's signed Stoneham spectrum $S_p$ (a nowhere-dense Cantor-like set of dimension $\log 2 / \log p$).

## 8. Provisional priority and novelty classification for Phase 18 (Tracks A–F)

To avoid unsubstantiated novelty claims, each Phase 18 component is classified into one of the four G3 categories, recognizing that **provisional** labels require independent human literature review:

| Phase 18 component | Provisional G3 classification | Primary precedents & exact differentiating scope |
