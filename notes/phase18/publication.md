# Publication assessment

**Recommendation.** Develop one focused manuscript under the provisional title *Synthetic Arithmetic Expansions: Normality, Denominator Retirement and Programmable Discrepancy*. Its main question is what distribution can be forced by specifying rational states at sparse arithmetic positions. Lead with the valuation switch and the cubic realization of every ordinary discrepancy limit. These results share the same denominator arithmetic, complete-period counts, and treatment of unfinished prefixes. The binary cubic example provides a short worked proof before the general fixed-support theorem. [Principal family](principal.md) · [Programmed limits](programming.md)

The result with the shortest independent statement is the exact change-of-discrepancy formula for translations. It gives a converse from the single test point zero and converts previous discrepancy-deficit estimates into sharp uniform perturbation rates. It belongs in the same manuscript as the transfer result that explains when arithmetic remainders may be discarded. Put the construction of a positive normal sum with infinitely many denominator primes beside the positive rational-total counterexample: together they identify the extra arithmetic uniformity that summation needs. [Translations](translations.md) · [Infinite sums](summation.md)

The finite polynomial closure results involve a different leading-coefficient calculation. They merit a companion paper if the generality over earlier normal-ring constructions and the benefit over Paper I survive specialist priority review. The logarithm grouping delays and the functional-algebra obstruction fit the continuing classical-denominator project; a separate classical digit-distribution paper would require information about the orbit of the full constant. The exact decompositions of ln 2 and π are appropriate applications illustrating this boundary. [Algebra](algebra.md) · [Positive Taylor groups](prime-delay.md) · [Functional identities](functional-obstruction.md)

The all-shift Legendre approximation to π is a concrete link with the Phase 17 paper on classical denominators. It merits inclusion there as an arithmetic theorem even while the distribution of its numerators stays open. The Lambert translation-rate corollary belongs with the sharp translation law in the synthetic paper. [Phase 17 transfer results](phase17-transfer.md)

Historical positioning matters. Normal Liouville numbers, normal-number rings, symbolic realizations of invariant measures, and deterministic translations have substantial antecedents. Cite the original results and distinguish existence, explicit coefficients, finite-prefix bounds and evidence for the particular arithmetic family. Before external submission, compare the quantitative constants and hypotheses with Kano's numerator construction and Vandehey's applicable range, and submit the priority assessment and nonlinear arguments to a specialist. The project term for the grouping mechanism remains descriptive. [Source comparison](sources.md) · [Phase 17 publication roadmap](../../docs/publication-roadmap.md)

The research notes are the publication deliverable at this checkpoint. No journal submission, manuscript release, DOI or priority claim accompanies them.
