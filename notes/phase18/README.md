# Phase 18. Synthetic arithmetic expansions

For an integer radix $b\ge2$, an off-radix prime $p$, and $t$ the order of $b$ modulo $p$, insert the coefficient $p^{-s v_p(k)}$ at position $tk^d$, for $k\ge1$. Here $v_p(k)$ is the exponent of $p$ in $k$, $d\ge2$ and $s\ge0$ are integers. The value at $s=0$ has limiting extreme discrepancy one. Every positive $s$ gives a base-$b$ normal number. This holds along every observation prefix. For $b=2,p=3,s=1,d=3$ the discrepancy is $O(N^{-1/3})$. The denominator and qualitative switch have Lean proofs. The rate has a written proof. [Formal statements](../../NormalityChecker/RetirementDenominator.lean) · [Proof and worked family](principal.md)

## Results and their evidence

- **Design by rational grouping.** A finite geometric group cancels its repunit denominator exactly. With a retained prime, the reduced denominator after any shift is $p^{s\lfloor\log_p K\rfloor}$ for the last inserted index $K\ge1$, and one before the first insertion. The deepest layer has $\lfloor K/p^{\lfloor\log_p K\rfloor}\rfloor$ contributing indices. Positive groups can fill every raw position even when the reduced schedule is sparse. The group sum, bounded-multiplier infinite version, and exact prefix arithmetic are kernel-verified. The note proves infinite raw prime support for unbounded group lengths under its numerator bound. [Geometric identities](../../NormalityChecker/Phase18Synthetic.lean) · [Denominators](principal.md#exact-reduced-denominators)
- **Quantitative convergence.** For $d>s+1$, complete-period counts give $O(N^{-s/d}+N^{-(d-s-1)/d}+(1+\log N)N^{1/d-1})$. The order-lifting calculation handles proper subgroups. An incomplete-period character-sum argument enlarges the elementary power-saving range to $d>1+s/2$. These are written estimates with constants for fixed parameters. General sparsity with slowly growing pole depth can suffice even when complete periods do not fit. [Period proofs and comparison with Vandehey](principal.md#complete-periods-and-the-unfinished-gap)
- **Programmable limits.** For every $\delta\in[0,1]$, positive rational coefficients on a cubic schedule give $D_N=\delta+O_{b,p}(N^{-1/3})$. Rational states can also realize every ordered pair of lower and upper extreme discrepancies and the nonempty compact connected sets of invariant circle measures as weak limit sets. The sharp cubic estimate is proved directly on intervals. Uniform computability at rational floor boundaries uses a modified mask. A separate long-gap construction gives Liouville numbers with every full discrepancy limit, computably for computable $\delta$. [Proof and example](programming.md) · [Liouville realizations](liouville-realization.md)
- **Sharp numerical translation law.** The largest possible value of $|D_N(x+z)-D_N(x)|$ over real $x$ is exactly $1-D_N(z)$, attained at zero. The finite identity, the circle tolerance bound, radix-primary shifts, and the finite sparse perturbation bound have Lean proofs. The note classifies numerical preservers, gives the uniform $O(\log(2N)/\sqrt N)$ binary example, and derives positive and negative determinism results for the previous Lambert and signed-shell benchmarks. Rauzy's entropy characterization is an external input. [Sharp maximum in Lean](../../NormalityChecker/SharpTranslation.lean) · [Preservers and arithmetic remainders](translations.md)
- **Algebra.** Distinct retained primes give jointly normal components under a common sparsity bound. For positions $tk^d$, all powers through $\min(s,d-1)$ are jointly normal. On the factorial schedule every nonconstant rational polynomial of the same normal Liouville number is normal. An affine adjustment of one independent prime axis yields normal products when $1/d+1/e<1$. For the unmodified square of the flagship cubic example, a written coverage theorem and two exact 21-layer computations give upper discrepancy below $2.94\times10^{-10}$. Whether this square is normal remains open. [Polynomial and product proofs](algebra.md) · [Independent computation](../../scripts/phase18_review_algebra.py)
- **Infinite sums.** One positive normal binary sum over unbounded prime support has discrepancy $O((\log N)^{-1/2})$. Complete-period grids at 3 survive the introduction of every other prime. Conversely, positive rational multiples of jointly normal generators can sum to any positive rational. Lean verifies existence of the multipliers and their prescribed total. Mean-displacement and universal-preserver obstructions apply to fixed tails, while moving partial sums can supply valid positive coverage or normality. [Infinite-sum theorems](summation.md) · [Formal scaling theorem](../../NormalityChecker/PositiveSummation.lean)

## Classical constants and limitations

There are exact partitions of the binary Taylor series of $\log 2$ and the hexadecimal BBP series of $\pi$ into components with joint uniformity on every finite subcollection. There is also a partition of the logarithm into finite dyadic groups. For any such positive grouping, the completion index of the group containing the term at prime $p$ is at least $(1/(1+\log 2)-o(1))p\log p$. The unfinished pieces retain exactly the odd denominator of the original shifted Taylor prefix. The Taylor series for $e$ has no finite positive dyadic group with greatest index at least three. [Ray partitions](summation.md) · [Dyadic and factorial groups](classical.md) · [Prime delay](prime-delay.md)

A finite list of ray generating functions with polylogarithmically many occupied exponents through $N$ cannot generate the specified polylogarithm, BBP, exponential, nonintegral binomial or Apéry generating functions by rational-function weights, differentiation and polynomial operations. This obstruction is proved before evaluating the functions. The literal BBP and Bellard prefixes of $\pi$ have reduced log-denominator rates $3/2$ and $1$ per binary shift. For every positive rate threshold there is a fixed arctangent identity whose rounded Chebyshev approximants have smaller upper rate along the selected shifts. This still requires control of the orbit across shifts. The stronger all-prefix digit-distribution conclusions for the full classical constants remain open. [Functional obstruction](functional-obstruction.md) · [Classical evidence](classical.md) · [Dynamic localization threshold](summation.md#what-happens-on-approaching-the-full-logarithm)

The completed Phase 17 results give linear shadowing windows for π, sharp translation rates throughout the multiplicative Lambert family, and a recognition obstruction for discrepancy preservers. At binary shift 600 two independent arctangent identities give rational models with at least 261 and 213 bits of shifted accuracy. [Phase 17 transfers and proofs](phase17-transfer.md)
