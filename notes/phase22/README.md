# Phase 22 — Digit-Frequency Classification, Arithmetic Operations and the Next Distributional Frontier

Phase 22 advances all four standing research objectives ([`docs/research-objectives.md`](../../docs/research-objectives.md)) across ten research and infrastructure tracks, adds three new kernel-verified Lean 4 modules (+123 named public theorems and lemmas, bringing the library to 1,594 public theorems and 2,001 audited declarations), establishes the permanent 50-constant benchmark suite ([`docs/benchmarks/progress.md`](../../docs/benchmarks/progress.md)), registers ten new permanent results ([`GF-19`](../../docs/results.md#gf-19)–[`GF-28`](../../docs/results.md#gf-28)), and drafts two candidate manuscripts (**Paper XI** and **Paper VII v2.0**) awaiting owner release approval.

Every research note, exact-arithmetic script, benchmark specification, and candidate manuscript was independently audited by a cross-provider referee (Claude Opus 5.5 ↔ Gemini 4 Neon). **Cross-model review is not independent human peer review.**

1. **Strict radix-power kernel inclusion $K_b \subsetneq K_{b^k}$ ($k \ge 2$):** Corrected summary prose in [`GF-18`](../../docs/results.md#gf-18), [`notes/phase21/README.md`](../phase21/README.md), and [`docs/survey.md`](../../docs/survey.md); kernel-verified in [`RadixPowerTower.lean`](../../NormalityChecker/RadixPowerTower.lean) (`strict_kernel_inclusion`).
2. **Theorem U non-Liouville scope:** Corrected [`GF-15`](../../docs/results.md#gf-15), [`notes/phase21/multivariate-and-dense-allprime.md`](../phase21/multivariate-and-dense-allprime.md), and [`docs/survey.md`](../../docs/survey.md) to state explicitly that non-Liouville status and finiteness of $\mu$ are open for Theorem U (while Phase 22 Track C4 constructs explicit non-Liouville all-prime normal numbers with exact $\mu = \lambda$ and $\mu = p$).
3. **Complete proof of Lemma S (Theorem S):** Proved from scratch in [`partitions-carry-and-lemma-s.md`](partitions-carry-and-lemma-s.md) using a self-contained uniform Selberg upper-bound sieve across all shifts $H \le N$, removing the Phase 21 sketch gap in [`GF-16`](../../docs/results.md#gf-16).
4. **External Erdős–Borwein reconciliation:** Reconciled Mira Cult's pinned positive-word-density claim (`Mira-Cult/math` commit `1498319`) cleanly in [`GF-18`](../../docs/results.md#gf-18) and [`notes/phase21/classical-barriers.md`](../phase21/classical-barriers.md) with strict external-input scoping.
