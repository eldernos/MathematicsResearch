## What is not covered

- **Code** keeps the [MIT licence](LICENSE). This includes the Lean files, the Python, shell and other scripts, and the `*.lean`, `*.py` and `*.sh` files inside manuscript directories.
- **The port of Trevor Morris's Weyl criterion** keeps its [Apache-2.0 licence](NormalityChecker/Analysis/LICENSE-Weyl).
- **Earlier releases are not relicensed.** The files released at `manuscripts-v1.0.0` and `manuscripts-v1.1.0`, and the archived v1.1 directories `papers/01-stoneham-polynomials/` through `papers/05-classical-denominators/` with their review records, remain under the terms that applied when they were released.
- **Third-party material** is excluded. This includes quoted passages, cited works, and the content of public notes by other people (for example Mira Cult's and Captain Sude's). It belongs to its authors and is subject to their rights.
- The repository's other documentation, research notes and portal pages are not designated by this file. They remain covered by the repository's [MIT licence](LICENSE).
